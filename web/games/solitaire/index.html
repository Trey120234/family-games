How to play

Move every card up to the 4 piles at the top right, one pile per suit, starting with the Ace and going up to the King.

To move a card: tap it (it lifts up with a gold edge), then tap where it should go. Tap it again to put it back down. You can also drag a card there with your finger.

Shortcut: double-tap a card to send it straight up to its pile at the top.

In the 7 columns, a card can go on one that is one higher and the other color, like a red 6 on a black 7. Only a King can go in an empty column.

Tap the deck at the top left to turn over 3 new cards. Only the top one can be played. When the deck runs out, tap it to start it again.

Stuck? Undo takes back your last move. Every deal can be won.
