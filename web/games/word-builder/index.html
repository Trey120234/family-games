How to play

There are 7 hidden words: three with 4 letters, two with 5, one with 6, and one with 7.

Spell them using the seeds at the bottom. Tap the seeds in order to plant them in the pots, then tap Enter. Each seed can be used once per word.

Found words bloom into orange flowers. Find all 7 to finish the puzzle.

Tap Mix to shuffle the seeds, or Hint to show one letter for 1 coin.

Coins are shared by all the games. Tap the coin button at the top to see how many you have or add more.
