How to play

Fill every empty square so that each row, each column and each box (the areas with thick lines) has every number exactly once.

Easy is a small 4×4 grid with the numbers 1 to 4. Medium is 6×6 with 1 to 6. Hard and Very hard are the full 9×9 grid with 1 to 9.

Tap a square, then tap a number below. A number that can't be right turns red.

Turn on Notes to jot small reminder numbers in a square. Erase clears a square and Undo takes back your last change.

Stuck? Hint fills in one square for you, for 1 coin. Every puzzle has exactly one answer, and you never need to guess.

Coins are shared by all the games. Tap the coin button at the top to see how many you have or add more.
