How to play

Grow the hidden word. Type a word into the pots, then tap Enter.

Pick 4, 5 or 6 letters, or Mix for a surprise. Longer words get more tries.

After each try, something grows in every pot:

Orange flower - right letter, right spot
Blue bud - in the word, wrong spot
Gray stone - not in the word

Stuck? Tap Hint to show one letter for 1 coin.

Coins are shared by all the games. Tap the coin button at the top to see how many you have or add more.
