How to play

Clear the board by removing matching tiles in pairs. Tap a tile, then tap its match.

+

You can only use a tile that is free: nothing on top of it, and nothing touching its left or right side. Tiles you can't use yet look a little darker.

Stuck? Hint shows a matching pair for 1 coin, Shuffle mixes the tiles, and Undo takes back your last pair. Every board can be cleared.

Each new board is a different shape: Pyramid, Diamond, Castle, Bridge and Flower.

Coins are shared by all the games. Tap the coin button at the top to see how many you have or add more.
