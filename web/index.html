Games

Get the app

Install Games on an Android phone or tablet. It updates itself and works without internet.

Download for Android

Downloading… When it's done, open Files → Downloads and tap Games.apk to install.

After it downloads, open the file to install. Your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser first.

Put Games on your iPhone

Add Games to your home screen. It opens like an app, updates itself and works without internet.

  1. Open this page in Safari, then tap the Share button . If you don't see it, tap the ••• button first.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Games is now on your home screen.
